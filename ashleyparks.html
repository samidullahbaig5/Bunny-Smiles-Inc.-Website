Ashley Parks

Ashley was one of the three technicians a part of BSI's "Maintenance Unit" to visit the K9 Storage Facility to reprogram the animatronics for the relocation of Bon's Burger's, along with Hillarly and Kevin on July 12th, 1978. While at the bunker, Ashley and her technicians would play around in the facility instead of working for the most part, playing with the stored merchandise, building box forts, and drawing phalluses on the walls.
